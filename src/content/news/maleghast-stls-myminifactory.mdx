The official miniatures for [Magnagothica: Maleghast](/games/maleghast/) just went up on MyMiniFactory. [Taiga Miniatures](https://www.taigaminiatures.com/) sculpted them in an official collaboration with Tom Bloom, Kickstarted them in August, and the STLs started landing on 22 September.

**[See the Maleghast collection on MyMiniFactory](https://www.myminifactory.com/users/TaigaMiniatures/collection/magnagothica-maleghast)**

## What's in it

Every house has a full warband at **$66**:

- C.A.R.C.A.S.S.
- Goregrinders
- Deadsouls
- Gargamox
- Igorri
- Abhorrers
- Steeplewracks
- Hellminth
- Volvadani
- Mercenaries

There are also single units if you only want to fill a gap or try a house out before committing.

They're 30mm, pre-supported, and test-printed for resin. They're licensed for personal use only, so no printing them to sell.

## Why this matters

Maleghast has been around since October 2023 with no official models at all. It was designed for virtual tabletop first, and everyone playing it in person has been kitbashing or proxying. That still works fine. The game doesn't care what's on the board. But the Kickstarter raised over $200,000 from 867 backers, which says a lot of people wanted the real thing.

The rules are still $6.66 on [itch.io](https://tombloom.itch.io/magnagothica-maleghast), and all you need to play is a chessboard. If you want to know more about the game itself, the [Maleghast page](/games/maleghast/) covers how it plays and all nine houses.
