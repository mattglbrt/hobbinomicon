CMON started in 2001 as CoolMiniOrNot.com, a site where people posted photos of painted miniatures for others to vote on. It moved into board and tabletop games in 2011, and it's listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.

In the directory, CMON makes [A Song of Ice and Fire: Tabletop Miniatures Game](/games/a-song-of-ice-and-fire/). It developed the game with Dark Sword Miniatures and launched it on Kickstarter in July 2017. CMON keeps the game's free rulebook and seasonal card updates on its [ASOIAF site](https://asoiaf.cmon.com/).
