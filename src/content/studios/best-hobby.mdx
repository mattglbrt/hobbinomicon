Best Hobby (Best Hobby LLC) publishes [Zeo Genesis](/games/zeo-genesis/), a scalable skirmish game of big armored suits led by [Andy Chambers](/people/andy-chambers/), with [Gav Thorpe](/people/gav-thorpe/) as narrative lead. The game was crowdfunded on Gamefound.

The company says it spent four years building its own plastics capacity, and it makes its hard plastic models in the US.
