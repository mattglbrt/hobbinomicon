Irregular Wars Wargaming publishes the rules of [Nic Wright](/people/nicholas-wright/). In its own words, it "was born from a desire to play something different": historical games "too niche to be commercial," and fantasy games not tied to a miniatures range. It published the folk horror skirmish game [Devilry Afoot](/games/devilry-afoot/) and the fantasy mass-battle rules [Fantastic Battles](/games/fantastic-battles/), and its shop sells rules from both Irregular Wars and Ganesha Games.
