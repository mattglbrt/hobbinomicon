Monster Fight Club makes [Cyberpunk RED: Combat Zone](/games/cyberpunk-red-combat-zone/), a skirmish miniatures game made with R. Talsorian Games and built on its [RE]action System. It followed that with Cyberpunk Edgerunners: Combat Zone, a standalone acrylic-standee starter made with CD Projekt RED that works with the original game.

The studio sells its games, miniatures and terrain through its own store, and puts the Combat Zone rules and a long list of scenarios up as free downloads. Alongside Combat Zone it makes the Monster Scenery terrain range, and its store also carries Borderlands: Mister Torgue's Arena and The Witcher products.
