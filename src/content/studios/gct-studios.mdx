GCT Studios makes [Bushido](/games/bushido/), a two-player fantasy skirmish game set in the Jwar Isles. It sells the miniatures, faction starter sets and the printed Risen Sun rulebook through its own shop, and puts the full rules, errata, reference sheets and tournament pack up as free downloads.

The studio stays close to its players. Its developers post on the official Discord, playtesters called Retainers test new profiles, and winners of the Grand Masters tournament have helped design new models.
