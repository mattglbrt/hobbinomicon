Freecompany d.o.o. calls itself "a small, dedicated development team," based in Ljubljana. Its flagship is [Eldfall Chronicles](/games/eldfall-chronicles/), a high-fantasy setting that pairs a skirmish miniatures wargame with a dungeon crawl, and the miniatures work in both. [Žiga Gantar](/people/ziga-gantar/) founded the company and is its CEO and creative director. [Tjaša Gaber](/people/tjasa-gaber/) is co-creator and leads creative and editing.
