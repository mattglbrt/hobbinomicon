Victrix makes historically accurate 28mm hard-plastic miniatures. It started more than 25 years ago in London as one person making figures at home, and it's still family run, now based in the Lake District.

They publish the English edition of [Pillage](/games/pillage/), Guillaume Rousselot's early medieval skirmish game, and make the warbands, terrain, and Introductory Set built for it.
