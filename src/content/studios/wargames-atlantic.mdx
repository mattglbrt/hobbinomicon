Wargames Atlantic makes multipart plastic miniatures, with warehouses in the USA and the UK. Its plastics are "Made on Both Sides of the Pond": the US market's are made in the USA, and everyone else's in the UK.

It publishes the Second Edition of [The Barons' War](/games/the-barons-war/), [Andy Hobday](/people/andy-hobday/)'s medieval skirmish game, along with the Starter Set, the King John sourcebook, and the plastic knights, serjeants and levy made for it. Hobday is the company's Creative Director.

It also sells the PDF books for [Matthew Sutton-O'Connor](/people/matthew-sutton-oconnor/)'s [Brethren](/games/brethren/), a 28mm skirmish game for the Ancient and Dark Age eras.
