Bandai's hobby products, Gunpla included, come from BANDAI SPIRITS CO., LTD. **Bandai Tabletop Games** is the collective name Bandai Hobby uses for its tabletop game titles.

Its miniatures game is [Gundam Assemble](/games/gundam-assemble/), a tactical game played with two-inch Gunpla. The Starter Set reaches general retail on 31 October 2026.
