CHASM is the label [Tom Bloom](/people/tom-bloom/) puts his own games out under, all sold through his itch.io page. The wargame is [Magnagothica: Maleghast](/games/maleghast/), a necromancer skirmish game played on an 8×8 grid. Alongside it there's *Games for Freaks*, a zine that adds new houses and rules to Maleghast, and CAIN, a horror TTRPG.
