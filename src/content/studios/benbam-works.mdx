benbam works is the label [Ben Baez](/people/ben-baez/) sculpts under. Its first release is [Bellwoken](/games/bellwoken/), a 28mm fantasy army sold as STL files, which funded on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/bellwoken/bellwoken-a-new-whimsical-army-set-for-your-wargames) in June 2026.
