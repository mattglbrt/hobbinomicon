The Ministry of Gentlemanly Warfare is a game-writing and publishing partnership owned by [Craig Cartmell](/people/craig-cartmell/) and [Charles Murton](/people/charles-murton/). The two started writing together when Osprey commissioned In Her Majesty's Name, their narrative skirmish series.

Since then the Ministry has published [Blood Eagle](/games/blood-eagle/), its Dark Ages skirmish game, along with Daishō, set in mythical Japan, the Victorian horror supplement IHMN Gothic, and the fantasy game Thud & Blunder. Blood Eagle, Daishō and In Her Majesty's Name share one core engine. The Ministry's blog keeps a large stock of free material for all of them.
