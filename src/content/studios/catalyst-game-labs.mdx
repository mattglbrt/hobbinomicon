Catalyst Game Labs develops and publishes [BattleTech](/games/battletech/), including its fast-play version, Alpha Strike. It also runs the Shrapnel fiction magazine, the official BattleTech novel line, and a worldwide Demo Team program.

Its other games include *Shadowrun* and *Leviathans*. Catalyst sells direct through its [own store](https://store.catalystgamelabs.com/).
