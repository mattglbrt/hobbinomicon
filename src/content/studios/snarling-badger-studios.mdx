Snarling Badger Studios was founded by two content creators, [Adam Loper](/people/adam-loper/) of Tabletop Minions and [Vince Venturella](/people/vince-venturella/). The studio says it makes "fun, story-driven miniature wargames that are well-polished and reasonably priced."

Every game is mini-agnostic, so you play with models you already own. In order:

- [Reign in Hell](/games/reign-in-hell/) (2021), the first one: demon skirmish with a campaign system.
- [Space Station Zero](/games/space-station-zero/) (2022): a stranded crew works through a branching campaign inside an ancient space station, solo, co-op or head to head.
- [Majestic 13](/games/majestic-13/) (2023): covert operatives hunt aliens, solo, co-op or head to head.
- [Deth Wizards](/games/deth-wizards/) (2024): you play the villain, a necromancer raising an undead horde.
- [Reign in Iron](/games/reign-in-iron/) (2025): the stand-alone sequel to Reign in Hell, demons against robots.
- [Repent! Ye Foolish Gods](/games/repent-ye-foolish-gods/), the newest: solo and co-op, about killing the dark gods.

Rules are sold through WargameVault, and there's a [merch shop](https://shop.snarlingbadger.com) for shirts.
