ZombieSmith makes the Quar, a line of miniatures and games set in the world of Alwyd, where the Long War has been going for 700 years. Its games include [This Quar's War](/games/this-quars-war/), the 28mm skirmish game *Clash of Rhyfles*, and *Songs of Our Ancestors*, a skirmish game based on Ganesha Games' *Flying Lead*. The first edition of This Quar's War came out in 2009. The current edition, The Long War, carries a Grimsical Games copyright.

The Quar lore, maps and most of the rulebooks live on the [Quar Historical Archive](https://rhyfler.com/), where many of the older books are free PDFs.
