Grey For Now Games is run by [Graham Davey](/people/graham-davey/). In its own words, "'Us' is actually just 'Me'." It makes the samurai skirmish game *Test of Honour*, the WWII commando game *02 Hundred Hours*, and [Guards of Traitor's Toll](/games/guards-of-traitors-toll/), a semi-cooperative game about policing a fantasy city. It also sells a range of Traitor's Toll terrain.
