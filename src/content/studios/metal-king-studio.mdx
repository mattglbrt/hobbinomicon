Metal King Studio is the one-person operation behind Relicblade, a tabletop tactics-RPG of heroic adventurers exploring a dying world. The whole catalog (rules, the metal miniature line, character and upgrade cards, supplements) is designed and produced by [Sean Sutter](/people/sean-sutter/).

Sutter also makes [Sludge](/games/sludge/), a miniature-agnostic fantasy wargame set in a black powder age, sold as PDF rules alongside resin miniatures, conversion heads and printable digital kits.
