Atomic Mass Games says it's made up of "veterans from the tabletop miniatures games industry." Its first game was [Marvel: Crisis Protocol](/games/marvel-crisis-protocol/), published in 2019.

Since then the studio has taken over development of Star Wars: X-Wing, Star Wars: Legion, and Star Wars: Armada. In June 2023 it launched [Star Wars: Shatterpoint](/games/star-wars-shatterpoint/), a skirmish game.

Both of its skirmish games are licensed, from Marvel and from Star Wars, and they're sold through [Asmodee's store](https://store.asmodee.com/collections/atomic-mass).
