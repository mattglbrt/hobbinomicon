Electi Studio is a California-based publisher of games and books, founded by Gregory Horton, the editor of the wargaming magazine *Blaster*. It publishes [Mike Hutchinson](/people/mike-hutchinson/)'s fantasy mass battle game [Hobgoblin](/games/hobgoblin/) under license, along with other miniatures rules and the *Blaster* volumes.
