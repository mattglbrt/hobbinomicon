Little Wars TV is a YouTube channel about historical tabletop wargaming, with battle reports, hobby tutorials and big refights of battles like Gettysburg, Agincourt and Trafalgar.

It publishes [Ravenfeast](/games/ravenfeast/), a short Viking Age skirmish game written as a way into historical wargaming. The full book is a free PDF, with free scenarios, paper figures and supplements on [ravenfeast.com](https://www.ravenfeast.com/).
