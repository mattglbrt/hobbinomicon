Under the Dice started as a small zine and has grown into a full-size gaming and hobby magazine. It covers RPGs, tabletop wargames, music, art, homebrew rules, and movies, and it runs Under the Dice Fest.

It publishes [Flames of Orion](/games/flames-of-orion/), a mech skirmish game written by [Stephen Hupfer](/people/stephen-hupfer/). The rules are sold as a PDF on [itch.io](https://underthedice.itch.io/) and as a physical book on the [Big Cartel store](https://underthedice.bigcartel.com/), alongside resin Mechs, unit sheet pads, and merch.
