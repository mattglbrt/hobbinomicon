Goblin King Games makes [Moonstone](/games/moonstone/), a fantasy skirmish game set on the fairytale isle of Tauber. It sells the resin miniatures, the rulebook and card decks through its own shop, and puts the core rules and every character card up as free downloads.

At UK Games Expo 2023, Moonstone won both the Judges' Choice and the People's Choice awards for Best Miniatures Range and Best Miniatures Rules.

The studio also runs the Goblin Scheme, a volunteer program for players who run demo days and tournaments at their local store or club, and supports organised play through Longshanks.
