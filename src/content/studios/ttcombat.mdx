TTCombat started out as a news and reviews website that sold a few homemade products to cover its running costs. The response to those first designs changed the plan, and the company now makes MDF terrain kits alongside its own miniatures games. They're based in Indian Queens, in England.

Their games include [Carnevale](/games/carnevale/), Dropzone Commander, Dropfleet Commander, RUMBLESLAM, Half Tilt, Home Raiders, Relics, and Space Swarm. In their own words, the settings run "from fantasy wrestling rings, through Lovecraftian inspired Venetian horror, all the way to ground and space battles in the far future."
