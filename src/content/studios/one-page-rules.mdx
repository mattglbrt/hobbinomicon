OnePageRules (OPR Games, based in Lisbon) makes free wargames that are quick to learn. Its games are created by [Gaetano Ferrara](/people/gaetano-ferrara/) and come in two settings. Grimdark Future is the sci-fi one, with Firefight, Star Quest, and Warfleets. Age of Fantasy is the fantasy one, with [Skirmish](/games/age-of-fantasy-skirmish/), Regiments, and [Quest](/games/age-of-fantasy-quest/).

The core rules and the free [Army Forge](https://army-forge.onepagerules.com/) list builder cost nothing. OPR makes its money elsewhere: a [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/onepagerules) from $5 a month, paid advanced rulebooks and expansions on [DriveThruRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/browse/pub/16079/onepagerules), and miniatures. OPR says it has designed over 1,000 sci-fi and fantasy miniatures since 2020, sold as STLs on Patreon and [MyMiniFactory](https://www.myminifactory.com/users/onepagerules), pre-printed on [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/shop/ONEPAGERULES), as some plastic kits, or as paper minis. The games themselves work with any miniatures.
