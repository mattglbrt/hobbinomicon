Quarantine Miniatures is the name [Brett Evans](/people/brett-evans/) puts his work out under, on itch.io as brettfp and on Instagram as @quarantine_miniatures. The main release is [Brawl Arcane 28](/games/brawl-arcane-28/), a wizard-duel skirmish game played on a chessboard, along with its free expansion *Beast Bash*.
