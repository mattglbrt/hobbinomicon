Kingdom Death is the company behind [Kingdom Death: Monster](/games/kingdom-death-monster/), run as Adam Poots Games, LLC by its creator, [Adam Poots](/people/adam-poots/). Alongside the core game it sells monster expansions, the Gambler's Chest, miniatures, limited-edition resin pieces, and Kingdom Death: Simulator, all through its [own store](https://shop.kingdomdeath.com/).
