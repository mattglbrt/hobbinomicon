Black Site Studios started in late 2015 as a custom terrain studio, making MDF gaming terrain, and says it is now the largest pre-painted terrain maker in the USA. It has since moved into miniatures and publishing. Its first game was [Don't Look Back](/games/dont-look-back/) by [Matt Burns](/people/matt-burns/) in 2020, and other games like *Lunar* followed. Burns also wrote its solo and co-op Robin Hood game, [Outlaws: The Curse of Sherwood Forest](/games/outlaws-the-curse-of-sherwood-forest/).
