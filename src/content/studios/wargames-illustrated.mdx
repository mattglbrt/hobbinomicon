Wargames Illustrated, or Wi, says it has "provided historical, sci-fi, and fantasy wargaming coverage since 1987." Alongside the magazine it publishes games, and its games page lists Andy Callan's *Never Mind the Billhooks*, *Valour & Fortitude*, and *Blam! Blam! Aargh!*, among others.

[Here's the Ruckus](/games/heres-the-ruckus/), [Mike Peters](/people/mike-peters/)' medieval skirmish game, came free with issue 437 in May 2024. WiPrime, its paid membership, gives digital access to rulesets and other content.
