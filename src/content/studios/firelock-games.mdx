Firelock Games makes historical miniatures games and sells them, along with the miniatures and ships, through its own webstore. Its best-known line is [Blood & Plunder](/games/blood-and-plunder/), a 28mm skirmish game set in the 17th-century Caribbean during the golden age of piracy, designed by [Mike Tuñez](/people/mike-tunez/). Its other game is [Port Royal](/games/port-royal/).

The studio runs a [Discord server](https://discord.gg/JhhJfYpBNa) and a Facebook group for each of its games, all linked from its [community page](https://firelockgames.com/pages/community).
