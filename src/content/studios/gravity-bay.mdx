Gravity Bay makes [Rapture](/games/rapture/), a 32mm skirmish game set in 2049. The studio has been working on it since 2014, and it funded on Kickstarter in December 2020 with 302 backers. The studio shipped the first packages to backers in October 2021 and launched the game officially on 12 March 2022. The rules are free to download in German and English, and the miniatures are sold through the studio's own [shop](https://www.rapture-shop.com/en).
