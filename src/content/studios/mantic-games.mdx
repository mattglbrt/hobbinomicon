Mantic Games makes [Halo: Flashpoint](/games/halo-flashpoint/), the officially licensed Halo miniatures game, which came out in November 2024. Its rules grew out of [Deadzone](/games/deadzone/), Mantic's own sci-fi skirmish game.

Deadzone and [Firefight](/games/firefight-mantic/) share Mantic's Warpath universe and its factions. Deadzone is the small one: a strike team of 5 to 15 models fighting on a 2-foot board of 3-inch cubes, now in its Third Edition. Firefight is the bigger battle game, with squads, tanks and airships on a 4-foot table, and its Second Edition rules and force lists all sit in one book, Command Protocols. Both games have free getting started rules, and Mantic keeps the latest rules for each on the Mantic Companion.

Mantic also makes [The Walking Dead: All Out War](/games/walking-dead-all-out-war/), a survival skirmish game based on the comics, which came back in 2024 after the licence was renewed.
