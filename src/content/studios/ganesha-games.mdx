Ganesha Games publishes fast, small-table miniatures rules that work with models you already own. Its best known game is [Song of Blades and Heroes](/games/song-of-blades-and-heroes/), the fantasy skirmish game by [Andrea Sfiligoi](/people/andrea-sfiligoi/), and the same engine runs many of its other titles. It also publishes Sfiligoi's solo and co-op game [Sellswords & Spellslingers](/games/sellswords-and-spellslingers/).

The studio sells its PDFs through its own site and Gumroad, and its printed books through Lulu.
