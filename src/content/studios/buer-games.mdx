Buer Games makes [1490 DOOM](/games/1490-doom/), the skirmish game where three-warrior Doom Companies climb above a rising fog of death. Cody Taylor is the co-founder, and Eric Michael Robertson is one of the game's creators.

The [store](https://buergames.com/) carries the core rulebook, the solo and co-op campaign books *The Fall of Rothenburg* and *The Gates of Rennes*, resin Doom Warriors, a modular castle, and STL versions of most of it. The starter set is *The Outpost at Meerbridge*. Free class rules, the FAQ, and a quick reference are on the [resources page](https://buergames.com/pages/1490-doom-resources).
