Spectre Miniatures makes "highly detailed, accurate and diverse" 28mm figures for modern warfare, along with 1/50 vehicles, guns and scenery. The figures are 3D printed, and it sells STL files too. The company ran its first Kickstarter in 2014 and was coming up on ten years old in 2023.

Its rules are [Spectre Operations](/games/spectre-operations/), with the Frontlines and Outbreak expansions and the historical spin-off *Spectre: Out of Time*. Its Lovecraftian skirmish game *Cosmic Horror* was Kickstarted in 2026. It also publishes *Aftermath*, a separate d100 post-disaster skirmish game, and its store carries games from [Black Site Studios](/studios/black-site-studios/).
