Corvus Belli is a Spanish miniatures studio, founded in 2001 in Cangas, Galicia, by [Fernando Liste](/people/fernando-liste/), [Carlos Torres](/people/carlos-torres/), and Pilar Rivero. They're still in Galicia, in Bueu, just across the water from Vigo.

Their flagship is [Infinity](/games/infinity/), the sci-fi skirmish game that came out in 2005 and is now on its fifth edition, N5. It's credited to four creators: Liste, Torres, [Gutier Lusquiños](/people/gutier-lusquinos/), and [Alberto Abal](/people/alberto-abal/). The game has ten main factions plus the Non-Aligned Armies, each split into sectorial sub-armies, and the rules are free.

Alongside Infinity they publish [Aristeia!](https://aristeia.com/) (a competitive arena game set in the same universe), [Infinity Defiance](https://www.corvusbelli.com/en/our-products/infinity-universe/defiance) (a co-op dungeon crawler for 1 to 4 players), and [Warcrow](https://warcrow.com/en) (a fantasy units-based wargame that launched in 2024).
