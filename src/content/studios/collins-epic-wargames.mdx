Collins Epic Wargames is run by Byron Collins and his fiancée, Kat, out of Williamsburg, Virginia. It publishes [Oasis: Tactical Salvage Reclamation](/games/oasis/), designed by [Dale Eadeh](/people/dale-eadeh/).

Collins started self-publishing in 2008 with the *Spearpoint 1943* series of WWII card and miniatures games. He then published *Polyversal*, a miniatures-agnostic sci-fi game designed by Ken Whitehurst, which was nominated for an Origins Award for Best Miniatures Game in 2022.
