Bishok Mini makes [Sector AL-IX](/games/sector-alix/), a solo and co-op sky pirate game in 12mm scale designed for 3D printing. It funded on Kickstarter in 2024, and the *Rise* expansion followed with a second campaign.

Everything is sold as digital files through [Bishok's MyMiniFactory store](https://www.myminifactory.com/users/Bishok): rulebooks and campaign books as PDFs, and ships, crews and terrain as STLs. The store also carries other lines outside the game, including 8mm starships, 10mm cyberpunk, 28mm RPG terrain, and epic-scale models.
