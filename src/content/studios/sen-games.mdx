SEN Games calls itself "a new company dedicated to the design and production of high quality miniatures wargames." Its flagship game is [FIRE! Modern Combat](/games/fire-modern-combat/), created by [Ricardo Ponz](/people/ricardo-ponz/) and written with [Aleix Gordo Hostau](/people/aleix-gordo-hostau/). SEN also sells the game's white metal miniatures, a few books, and comics.

Apart from some parts made elsewhere in the EU, SEN says its products are made in Spain.
