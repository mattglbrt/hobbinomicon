Spellcrow makes [Argatoria](/games/argatoria/), a fantasy rank-and-flank wargame played with 10mm resin miniatures. It sells the rulebook, army starters and unit blisters through its own webstore, and lists stockists in the US, the UK, Germany, Canada and several other European countries.

Its store also carries the Umbra Turris range in 28/32mm, conversion bits, and accessories and terrain. Spellcrow Ltd is registered in England and Wales.
