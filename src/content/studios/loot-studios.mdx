Loot Studios makes 3D-printable miniature files, sold as a subscription service and used in all kinds of tabletop games, especially Dungeons & Dragons. [Malediction](/games/malediction/) is its first published game, launched on Gamefound in January 2025. Every Malediction product comes with STL files for the miniatures, so you can print your own army.
