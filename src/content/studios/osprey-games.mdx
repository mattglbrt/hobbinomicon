Osprey Games is the games imprint of Osprey Publishing, part of Bloomsbury. It publishes [Frostgrave](/games/frostgrave/) and its supplements, and the rest of [Joseph A. McCullough](/people/joseph-a-mccullough/)'s d20 family, including Stargrave. It also publishes [The Doomed](/games/the-doomed/), [Chris McDowall](/people/chris-mcdowall/)'s horror-hunting skirmish game, and [Daniel Mersey](/people/daniel-mersey/)'s [Lion Rampant](/games/lion-rampant/), the medieval wargame behind [Dragon Rampant](/games/dragon-rampant/). Its free [gaming resources page](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/discover/gaming-resources/frostgrave-stargrave/) has the reference sheets and errata.
