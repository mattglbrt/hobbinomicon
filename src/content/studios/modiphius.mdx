Modiphius Entertainment publishes tabletop games, and on the Hobbinomicon it's the publisher of [Rangers of Shadow Deep](/games/rangers-of-shadow-deep/): the Standard Edition core book, the A Gathering of Heroes collection, and the Creature Card Deck. The game's designer, [Joseph A. McCullough](/people/joseph-a-mccullough/), still self-publishes most of the mission books.
