Modiphius Entertainment publishes tabletop games, and on the Hobbinomicon it's the publisher of [Rangers of Shadow Deep](/games/rangers-of-shadow-deep/): the Standard Edition core book, the A Gathering of Heroes collection, and the Creature Card Deck. The game's designer, [Joseph A. McCullough](/people/joseph-a-mccullough/), still self-publishes most of the mission books.

Modiphius also publishes [Ivan Sorensen](/people/ivan-sorensen/)'s [Five Leagues from the Borderlands](/games/five-leagues-from-the-borderlands/), from the Third Edition on, and its sci-fi sibling [Five Parsecs From Home](/games/five-parsecs-from-home/), and its own licensed [Fallout: Wasteland Warfare](/games/fallout-wasteland-warfare/), designed by [James Sheahan](/people/james-sheahan/).

It also publishes the licensed [The Elder Scrolls: Call to Arms](/games/elder-scrolls-call-to-arms/), designed by [Mark Latham](/people/mark-latham/) and set in Skyrim, with solo and co-op delves alongside head-to-head battles.
