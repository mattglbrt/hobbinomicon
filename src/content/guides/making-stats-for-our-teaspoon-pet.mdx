Generating a pet in Teaspoon is a d8 straight down the line, which took about as
long as reading this will.

Health came up a one, which is a rough start. Aesthetics a six, helped along by
the lipstick. Magic landed on a solid eight, so: magical shrimp. Intelligence a
three, minus one for being a crustacean. Danger an eight, which follows, because
a magical shrimp probably is dangerous.

I picked the nature rather than rolling it, on the grounds that the model was
already painted like a blue lobster and there is no point rolling for something
the miniature has already decided. Get mad if you want.

Being a shrimp also costs a d4 of extra hunger and a d4 of intelligence, which
came up a two. Lucky.
