Kingdom Death terrain prints well, and printing a table's worth of it is mostly
a scheduling problem rather than a hobby one. A plate takes hours you are not
painting in, so it wants starting before you sit down rather than instead of.

The part worth getting right is the cure. Rushing it leaves a piece that looks
finished and is still soft, and you find that out when you handle it or when
paint refuses to sit on it. Putting a piece back in the curing machine for
another pass costs nothing.

The butcher base here is a free file — search "butcher base" and it comes up.
It needed sanding around one edge, which is an outdoors job with a mask on.
