## What is Dragon Rampant

*Dragon Rampant* is a fantasy wargame by [Daniel Mersey](/people/daniel-mersey/), published by [Osprey Games](/studios/osprey-games/). It grew out of Mersey's medieval game *[Lion Rampant](/games/lion-rampant/)*, and Osprey describes it as "large-scale skirmish": a warband of a handful of units, each a block of individually based figures, fighting out the kind of battle you read about in fantasy fiction. Orc warbands, undead armies, barbarian tribes, monsters and sorcery.

There's no built-in setting and no official miniatures range. You build units from whatever fantasy figures you have, in 28mm or 15mm, and give them a troop type. Mersey has said he wanted it to feel like a modern throwback to 1970s and 1980s fantasy wargaming.

The first edition came out in December 2015 as a 64-page book in the Osprey Wargames series. Osprey lists Dragon Rampant as an Origins Award nominee. The **Second Edition** came out in October 2025 as a 232-page hardback, illustrated by Alan Lathwell and David Needham. Mersey says it keeps the original rules rather than rewriting them. The core plays much as it did, lined up with *Lion Rampant: Second Edition*, with more of everything around them.

## How it plays

- **Test to activate.** On your turn you pick a unit and an order (Move, Attack or Shoot) and roll two dice, needing that unit's number or better. Fail and your turn is over, so it's swingy and fast.
- **Order of play.** First you try to Rally any Battered units, then any units with Wild Charge have to charge, then you give your other orders.
- **Leaders help nearby.** Once per turn you can reroll one failed Move, Attack or Shoot test for a unit within 12" of your Leader, and units within 12" get +1 on Courage tests. A Battered Leader loses both.
- **Strength Points, not models.** A unit rolls 12 dice when it attacks or shoots while above half strength and 6 once it's down to half. Losses are Strength Points, so the number of figures on the base doesn't change the stats.
- **Courage.** A unit that takes losses tests Courage, minus one for every Strength Point lost so far. Fail and it retreats and becomes Battered, and Battered units only hit on 6s. Score 0 or less and it flees the table.
- **Magic by colour.** Spellcasters buy access to up to four colours of magic, drawing on a 20-spell spellbook with names like *You Shall Not Pass!* and *Sod-Off Spell!*

Standard games are now 30 army points, up from 24 in the first edition, with 24 and 36 as options. That works out to 4 to 12 units at 24 or 30 points and 6 to 18 at 36. It's all six-sided dice, plus one twelve-sided die if you use the optional unit traits.

## What changed in the Second Edition

Mersey wrote up the changes for [Osprey's blog](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/osprey-blog/2025/what-changed-in-dragon-rampant-second-edition):

- Leaders no longer roll a random trait. They get the Commanding trait automatically and can buy upgrades.
- The unit upgrades went from 16 to over 48, some renamed or recosted and many new.
- The spellcasting got what he calls a major overhaul, with a 20-spell spellbook.
- There's an expanded campaign system, more scenarios, a terrain chapter, and more sample warbands.

## How to start

1. **Get the Second Edition.** $24.50 for the PDF or $35 for the hardback, from [Osprey](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/dragon-rampant-second-edition-9781472868640/).
2. **Grab the free downloads.** Osprey's [resources page](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/discover/gaming-resources/other-wargames/) has the Second Edition [quick reference sheet](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/media/5xohrwr3/dragon-rampant-second-edition-quick-reference.pdf) and a [warband roster](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/media/2o0ppsvq/dragon-rampant-second-edition-roster.pdf), with a fillable version too.
3. **Use the minis you already have.** Any fantasy range works, in 28mm or 15mm. The book's sample warbands are there to help you build one around your collection.
