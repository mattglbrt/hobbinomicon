## What is Oasis

*Oasis: Tactical Salvage Reclamation* is a 6mm sci-fi wargame designed by [Dale Eadeh](/people/dale-eadeh/) and published by [Collins Epic Wargames](/studios/collins-epic-wargames/), in partnership with Death Ray Designs and Tabletop Terrain. It's Eadeh's first published game. He designed the miniatures too.

The setting starts in the year 2257, when a hole in space opens onto "a micro galaxy in the vast emptiness between galaxies." Its worlds are covered in the leftovers of long-gone alien civilizations, and Earth's corporations send armed expeditions through to strip them. Your force is one of those expeditions, and so is your opponent's.

The models are infantry, mechs, vehicles, aircraft and capital ships. Death Ray Designs is printing the miniatures and Tabletop Terrain is printing the game's scenery. The publisher says Oasis will come as a physical version, retail-ready sets, and digital STLs.

## How it plays

- **Two players, a 3' × 6' table.** The scale is 6mm (1/285).
- **Salvage is the objective.** Points of interest on the table mark where salvage might be buried. You dig it up, load it onto a transport, and get it off the board.
- **Two ways to win.** The game ends when one player extracts three pieces of salvage, or when one side's forces are destroyed.
- **Bid for initiative.** Each side has a pool of twenty command points. At the start of every turn you can secretly bid some of them to act first, but whatever you bid is gone from the pool you use to influence units and dice rolls.
- **Unit cards.** Every unit's moves, attacks and abilities are on its card.
- **Two-step combat.** Roll to see if the attack hits, then whether it has the power to damage or destroy the target. The official site puts it bluntly: "if an attack can penetrate a unit's armor, that unit is more than likely destroyed."

## How to start

1. **Late pledge on Gamefound.** The campaign launched on 21 July 2026, funded in 36 minutes, and raised over $193,000. The [Gamefound page](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/collins-epic-wargames/oasis-tactical-salvage-reclamation) is open for late pledges.
2. **Watch for the electronic release.** The publisher is aiming for no later than 31 October 2026.
3. **Read the gameplay overview.** The [official site](https://oasiswargame.com/gameplay) has a short rules preview and photos of games in progress, and the lore page has a Combatant Recognition Guide to the units.
4. **Play a demo.** Collins Epic Wargames is running demo games at Call to Arms in Williamsburg, Virginia, 9–11 October 2026.
