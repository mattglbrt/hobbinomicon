## What is Carnivore

*Carnivore* is "a cataclysmic dinosaur skirmish game" from [Buer Games](/studios/buer-games/), the studio behind [1490 DOOM](/games/1490-doom/). It's set after an extinction-level event. Food is scarce, and the big predators are forced into direct competition over what's left. The tagline is "Eat first. Die last."

Each player commands a single species of carnivorous dinosaur, leading a small pack onto a table where a rival pack and a herd of herbivores all converge on the same shrinking supply of meat. You win by eating more than your rival, not by killing them. Securing a carcass often matters more than wounding the other pack.

Buer pitches the miniatures as paleo-accurate, built from skeletal reference and modern reconstructions. They're 32mm scale, and most species run 5 to 9 inches long. The T. rex and Spinosaurus are the ones near 9.

## How it plays

- **Four phases.** A turn runs Drive, Movement, Fight, and Panic.
- **D10 against Guard.** An attacker rolls a D10 and adds its Fight value. The total is compared to the target's Guard.
- **Every fight is a risk.** Win, and the target rolls Guard checks equal to the difference between the totals. Lose, and the target Retaliates and you roll the Guard checks instead.
- **Facing matters.** Attack from the front arc and you risk Retaliate. Attack from the rear arc and the target's Guard checks get harder.
- **Panic.** Anything that takes damage tests in the Panic Phase. Fail, and its later Guard rolls get harder. A wounded animal can lose its nerve and run.
- **The prey fights back.** Herbivores have their own instinct rules. They watch their front arc, bolt when they spot you, and can turn and hurt a predator that comes at them badly.

Each species has a stat card called a Genus Slate. The full rulebook covers twelve species, eleven scenarios, and the EXTINCTION campaign, where every loss is permanent.

## How to start

1. **Try the free Quickplay.** The [Quickplay Guide](https://buergames.com/products/quickplay-guide) is free. It has illustrated rules, a first scenario called COLLAPSE, and printable Genus Slates for three predators and one prey species. Buer says it's two players, one herd, and about forty minutes.
2. **Gather the basics.** Ten-sided dice, a tape measure, a 2' × 2' surface, and some terrain. For the dinosaurs, Buer's own line is "Official miniatures if you have them, toys if you don't. The rules do not care."
3. **Get the rulebook.** The [core rulebook PDF](https://buergames.com/products/carnivore-core-rulebook-pdf) is $20. It's 100+ pages with the full rules, the Genus Slates, the scenarios, and the campaign.
4. **Print the minis.** The official miniatures are presupported STLs, $18 per species on the [Buer Games store](https://buergames.com/collections/carnivore-digital). There are STLs for meat tokens, a terrain set, and a jawbone measuring gauge too.
5. **Or grab a bundle.** The MESOPREDATOR bundle ($72) has the PDF, raptors, and Triceratops. MEGAPREDATOR ($72) has the PDF, a T. rex, Allosaurus, and Ankylosaurus. APEX ($200) is every species and all the terrain.

The store sells the rules and minis as digital files only. There's no physical rulebook or resin miniature listed there yet.
