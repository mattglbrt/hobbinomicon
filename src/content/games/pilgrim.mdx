## What is Pilgrim: The Haunted Frontier

*Pilgrim: The Haunted Frontier* is a solo and co-op skirmish game from [Buer Games](/studios/buer-games/), the studio behind [1490 DOOM](/games/1490-doom/). Buer calls it "a solo and co-op game of colonial folk horror."

It's autumn 1605. Six ships left England together for the New World, and one morning yours wakes up stranded on the rocks with no sign of the others. From the official site: "This is not the shore the maps foretold." The forest behind the beach is huge and dark, the mist moves wrong, and something with antlers has been watching from the trees since you landed.

The full game has a campaign page on [Gamefound](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/buer-games/pilgrim-the-haunted-frontier). You can try it now with a free intro scenario.

## How it plays

- **Solo or co-op.** One to three players.
- **A party of survivors.** You pick your party from seven unique Pilgrims and lead them into the wilderness past the shore.
- **Defend the wreck.** The job is protecting the people still sheltering at the shipwreck and facing the horrors that hunt them.
- **Scenario play.** A scenario takes 60 to 120 minutes on average, per Buer Games.

The official miniature renders show the Pilgrims as figures in tall hats and hooded cloaks, carrying a musket, a lantern and a book, up against wraith-like figures and a huge antlered creature.

## How to start

1. **Download The Hollow Shore.** It's [free on the Buer Games store](https://buergames.com/products/the-hollow-shore-free-scenario-for-pilgrim-the-haunted-frontier): a 40-page PDF that teaches the rules and includes the first scenario.
2. **Print your first two models.** The same download has an STL for a Pilgrim and one for a Shade, which Buer calls the game's "haunting adversary". Buer says pre-supported versions are coming.
3. **Follow the campaign.** The full game has a campaign page on [Gamefound](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/buer-games/pilgrim-the-haunted-frontier).

The download also comes with a track from the upcoming Pilgrim soundtrack.
