## What is Brawl Arcane 28

*Brawl Arcane 28* is a wizard-duel miniatures game by [Brett Evans](/people/brett-evans/). Nearly three decades ago a meteorite fell and brought magic into the world, and not long after, the first Brawl Arcane was held in the city of Mirage. Now it's the 28th tournament, and wizards come from all over to settle scores, humiliate their rivals, and fight for fame in front of a baying crowd.

It's a two-player game. Each side is one wizard, who summons up to three minions during the fight, and the game ends when one wizard gets knocked out. It needs an 8×8 grid at minimum, so a regular chessboard works, and so does a Blood Bowl pitch or a D&D battlemat. Games take about 30 minutes.

The rules are **28mm and miniature agnostic**, and Brett wrote them as a jumping off point for conversions as much as a game. He's named Turnip28, The Weald, and Blood Bowl as the inspirations.

It was developed in public on itch.io through 2022 and hit **v1.0** in October that year.

## How it plays

Each player picks a **Magic Discipline**, which sets your wizard, their signature spell, and the minions they summon. Disciplines include the Necromancer, Mermancer, Green Witch, Artful Trickster, Astral Warlock, Draconic Magus, Flesh Transmuteur, and Noble Scholar.

Every model activates once a turn: a move and one action, then play passes back and forth. The twist is that **your spells are rolled at random each turn**. You might be stuck with Summon when what you really wanted was Shield, so a lot of the game is making the most of whatever the dice hand you. Magic Missile is always there as a fallback.

The crowd matters too. You earn **Crowd Favour** when a minion is destroyed, and spend it at the start of a turn.

Beyond a straight duel, the rules have **Events** to mix things up: grudge matches, a living arena, a crumbling arena, free-for-alls, beast hunts with tables for building your own beast, and a full Grand Tournament with its own sheet.

1. **Get the rules.** Pay what you want on [itch.io](https://brettfp.itch.io/brawl-arcane-28). Grab the free [Beast Bash](https://brettfp.itch.io/brawl-arcane-28-beast-bash) expansion while you're there.
2. **Grab a chessboard.** Anything 8×8 or bigger works.
3. **Build a wizard and three minions.** Four models a side. Any 28mm minis work, and the game is built for converting and kitbashing.
4. **Or buy a set.** [NeverRealm Industry](https://neverrealm.de/en/fantasy/brawl-arcane-28/) in Germany sells resin wizard-and-minion sets for several disciplines.
