## What is Sector AL-IX

*Sector AL-IX* is "a cloud pirate Tactical RPG playable in Solo/Coop for 3d printing in 12mm scale." It's made by [Bishok Mini](/studios/bishok-mini/), a studio in Toulouse, France, and the store page calls it a wargame/RPG at 12mm (1/150) scale.

You play a sky pirate captain and their fleet. Aboard makeshift anti-gravity ships, the goal is to pile up as much loot as you can, so you can keep your crew going and grow it by running missions. The enemy is the Compagnie du Nord, which has unlimited resources and will use them to stop you. Bishok's line is that it's "up to you to find the right strategy to evade them."

Everything is digital. The miniatures, ships and terrain are STL files, and the rulebooks and cards are PDFs in English and French. The game ran on Kickstarter from June 12 to July 12, 2024, and 211 backers pledged €12,121. A second campaign, the *Rise* expansion, drew 288 backers.

## How it plays

- **Solo or co-op.** The core game is built for one player or a team working together against the Compagnie du Nord.
- **Run a pirate fleet.** You take jobs, collect loot, and spend it to keep your crew and ships running.
- **Missions and campaigns.** The core bundle includes the *Troubled Sky* campaign book and an army book for Underdock 6.
- **Sandbox campaigns.** The *Redlight Campaign Book* moves the fight to Underdock 12, with piracy, smuggling, black markets and district conquest. It has 13 new missions, variant maps and modular battlefields, and it plays solo, co-op or multiplayer.
- **Built for printing.** The models are designed for resin (SLA) printing, with some pieces marked for FDM. Bishok notes that 12mm is the native scale, but like any STL you can scale it to suit.

## How to start

1. **Get the Core Game.** The [Core Game bundle](https://www.myminifactory.com/bundle/554/Sector-AL-IX-CORE-GAME) on MyMiniFactory is $80.99 for 23 items. It's the late pledge for the first Kickstarter: the French/English rules book, the *Troubled Sky* campaign book, the Compagnie du Nord faction pack, the Docker Clan, pirate boats, a dock kit, and more.
2. **Print it.** You need a 3D printer, or a friend with one. The files come supported and unsupported.
3. **Add expansions.** The [Rise expansion bundle](https://www.myminifactory.com/bundle/580/Sector-AL-IX-Rise-expansion) is $64.99 for 14 items, with new mission packs and vehicles. The [Redlight Campaign Book](https://www.myminifactory.com/object/3d-print-sector-alix-redlight-campaign-book-727789) is a 70-page PDF for $16.99, and it needs the Core Game to play.
4. **Or try it free.** Bishok says a few free parts are on MyMiniFactory if you want to test the models first.
