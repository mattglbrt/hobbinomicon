## What is When Nightmares Come

*When Nightmares Come: An Investigative Wargame of Supernatural Horror* is a solo and cooperative skirmish game from [Osprey Games](/studios/osprey-games/), written by [Patrick Todoroff](/people/patrick-todoroff/) and illustrated by Boris Groh. It's number 33 in the Osprey Wargames series, an 80-page softcover that came out on March 28, 2024 in the UK and April 30, 2024 in the US.

It's set in the modern world. You play a team of up to five paranormal vigilantes, self-taught occult specialists and monster hunters who call themselves the Nightwatch. They look into crimes that might have something supernatural behind them: waterfront murders, missing university students, museum thefts, mutilated pets. Osprey's pitch is that the monsters never went away. They just learned to hide better.

Osprey calls it an "open-world, open-miniature wargame." The terms in the book are kept generic on purpose so you can fit it to your own story, and you use whatever 28mm figures you have.

## How it plays

- **An Action Dice Pool.** Your hunters act with d6, d8, and d10. A 4+ after modifiers is a success, whether you're in a fight or a narrative scene. If you fail, you can try again with a different die.
- **Two Dice Bonus.** When an action fits a character's specialty, you sometimes roll two matching dice and keep the better one.
- **Enemies by Caste.** Enemy models use a single Action Die set by their Caste, their quality level, and need the same 4+. Osprey describes the combat as fast and bloody, with tiered enemies.
- **The d4 runs the tables.** Loot tables, event tables, and where enemies come onto the table all use a d4.
- **Narrative scenes and tactical engagements.** The book has a roleplaying element for non-combat challenges and dramatic encounters, using the same dice with an emphasis on quick resolutions. Separate chapters cover Narrative Scenes, Tactical Engagements, and Full Cases.
- **Build your hunters.** Character Creation comes early in the book, and Osprey lists flexible player classes among the core features. The Splendor of Fire review read them as a warrior, a wizard, and a tinker or mechanic.
- **Monsters in the Dark Spawn chapter.** A typical campaign uses three types of enemy, 30 models in all, plus one boss monster.

## How to start

1. **Get the book.** Osprey sells it for $25 in paperback, or $20 as a PDF or ePub and Mobi ebook, on the [When Nightmares Come product page](https://www.ospreypublishing.com/us/when-nightmares-come-9781472860040/).
2. **Read it all first.** The introduction recommends reading the whole rulebook before you make characters.
3. **Gather about 36 figures.** Osprey's introduction puts a typical campaign at around 36 miniatures in 28mm: your five hunters, 30 enemies in three types, and one boss.
4. **Set up a small table.** You need a 2' × 2' play area, a ruler, blast and teardrop templates, d4, d6, d8, and d10 dice, and some status tokens. Osprey says missions run under 90 minutes.
