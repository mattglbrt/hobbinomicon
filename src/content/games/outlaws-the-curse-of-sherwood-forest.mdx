## What is Outlaws

*Outlaws: The Curse of Sherwood Forest* is a solo and cooperative skirmish game written by [Matt Burns](/people/matt-burns/) and published by [Black Site Studios](/studios/black-site-studios/), the Don't Look Back people. It's Robin Hood with a dark fantasy twist. King Richard has been betrayed, Robin Hood has gone missing, and King John's curse has the land in its grip. The rest of the band fights on in Sherwood.

You play four members of Robin Hood's band, sometimes with Companions along, from loyal squires to animal allies. You're up against the Sheriff of Nottingham's forces and whatever lives in the wilds. Every game is built around narrative objectives, and there's no opponent: the players run the King's Men too.

It's miniature agnostic, and Black Site pushes kitbashing. Mix parts from whatever ranges you like to build your Outlaws, King's Men and forest horrors. Black Site also makes its own range of minis for it.

The game is on [Gamefound](https://gamefound.com/en/projects/black-site-studios/outlaws-the-curse-of-sherwood-forest) now. The campaign opened on 29 September 2026, hit its $10,000 goal in 1 hour and 8 minutes, and runs to 14 October. Rewards are listed for March 2027.

## How it plays

- **Skill pools of d8s.** Your relevant skill says how many D8s to roll, and each 5+ usually counts as a success. A Drained Outlaw rolls D6s instead. A Boosted one rolls D10s.
- **An event every round.** Each round opens with a roll on the adventure's event table, which pushes the story along.
- **Tokens from a bag.** Activation is drawn from an Initiative Bag. An Outlaw's token lets that character move and act. An enemy token means the players pick which of the King's Men acts, going for whatever makes sense for the story and gives the Outlaws a problem.
- **Push your luck.** Each Outlaw gets a free move and can try to chain Routine actions. Every extra one needs a harder Maneuver roll, and pushing too far builds Strain. Too much Strain leaves the Outlaw Fatigued and Drains their Skill Pool.
- **Know when to run.** The King's Men keep bringing reinforcements, so you have to decide whether to press on or sound the horn and live to fight another day.
- **A campaign.** Eight Outlaw classes with three skill trees each, plus experience, gear and magical relics that carry between adventures. The core book also has rules for sorcery, stealth and ambushes.

Games run 45 to 60 minutes on a 3' × 3' table, with D6s, D8s and D10s.

## How to start

1. **Read the free quickstart.** The [Preview Quickstart](https://blacksitestudio.com/products/outlaws-quickstart-preview) is $0 on the Black Site store. If you'd rather not check out, they'll give you the PDF on their [Discord](https://discord.gg/9VCUAxqCY4).
2. **Back it on Gamefound.** The Archive Master pledge is $85 for all the digital items. The Apprentice Outlaw pledge is $94 for the physical intro items, and Highwayman is $176 for all the physical launch items. There are Limited Edition versions of each with an alternate cover.
3. **Or pick the pieces you want.** The add-ons include the core rulebook ($40 in print, $20 as a PDF) and the **Secret of Sanctuary Hill** Narrative Starter Box ($65, or $35 digital). The box has the full core rules as a softback, 4 Outlaw and 4 King's Men resin minis, a linked narrative campaign, card terrain, a token sheet and dice. You can play the whole game with either the box or the hardback.
4. **Use your own minis.** You don't need Black Site's figures. If you want them, there are print-on-demand packs and all the core minis as STLs ($40).
