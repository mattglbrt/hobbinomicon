## What is Bolt Action

*Bolt Action* is [Warlord Games](/studios/warlord-games/)' World War II game, played with 28mm miniatures. It's Warlord's flagship, and it covers the whole war: Blitzkrieg, North Africa, the Russian Front, D-Day, the Pacific. You build a force of infantry, heavy weapons and vehicles from one nation and fight a battle on a table that's usually 6 × 4 ft.

The game was developed by [Alessio Cavatore](/people/alessio-cavatore/) and Rick Priestley. The first edition came out in 2012 and the second in 2016. The **Third Edition** arrived in September 2024, written by Cavatore and published with [Osprey Games](/studios/osprey-games/). It's a 320-page hardback with army lists for Great Britain, the USA, Germany, the Soviet Union and Imperial Japan.

Third Edition kept the Order Dice and the pin markers, and rebuilt a lot around them. Goonhammer's review calls it "a far more ambitious overhaul" than expected: new army building, new scenarios, reworked shooting and assaults, and a clarity pass over the whole book. Old second edition armies still work, but unit rules and points costs changed across the board.

## How it plays

- **Order Dice.** Every unit you have puts one die, in your army's colour, into a shared bag. Draw a die and whoever owns it activates one unit. Nobody knows who's going next. When the bag is empty, the turn is over, and a game usually runs six or seven turns.
- **Six orders.** The die's faces are the orders: **Fire** (shoot, no moving), **Advance** (move, then shoot with a penalty), **Run** (move faster, no shooting), **Ambush** (wait and react to the enemy later), **Rally** (shake off pins), and **Down** (hit the dirt and get harder to hurt).
- **Pin markers.** Units pick up pins as they take damage. Enough pins and a unit may refuse its orders or leave the table. Rallying costs you an activation you wanted for something else.
- **Company scale.** Third Edition moved from a single reinforced platoon to a company. You choose from six platoon types (infantry, engineers, heavy weapons, artillery, recce and tank), and Goonhammer says a typical 1,000-point list has about three of them.
- **Plain six-sided dice.** Everything else runs on d6s, a tape measure, and a few templates.

It sits closer to a war film than a simulation. Goonhammer calls it "firmly in the Hollywood action camp" and says a game is easy to finish in about three hours.

Warlord makes a huge range of plastic, metal and resin kits for it, but you can also use 28mm WWII miniatures from other makers.

## How to start

Prices below are from [Warlord Games' US store](https://us.warlordgames.com/collections/bolt-action).

1. **Get a Starter Set.** Both come with the Third Edition rules as an A5 rulebook, two small forces, a vehicle each, scenery, a double-sided mat, Order Dice, a dice bag, pin markers, templates and ten d6s.
   - [A Gentleman's War](https://us.warlordgames.com/products/bolt-action-starter-set-a-gentlemans-war-third-edition) ($149) is North Africa: 12 plastic British 8th Army infantry and 12 Afrika Korps, a Humber armoured car, an Sd.Kfz. 222/223 armoured car, and an MDF ruined North African house.
   - [Band of Brothers](https://us.warlordgames.com/products/bolt-action-starter-set-band-of-brothers-third-edition) ($152) is D-Day: 12 plastic US Airborne and 12 German Grenadiers, an M3A1 and an Sd.Kfz. 251/1 half-track, and a plastic ruined farmhouse. It adds a Field Manual that walks you through building, painting and introductory missions before the full rules.
2. **Already have WWII minis?** The [hardback rulebook](https://us.warlordgames.com/products/bolt-action-third-edition-rulebook-includes-book-figure) is $55 and comes with a resin special miniature of Medal of Honor recipient Francis S. Currey. There's also a [$35 PDF](https://us.warlordgames.com/products/digital-bolt-action-third-edition-rulebook-pdf) and a [$35 mini-rulebook](https://us.warlordgames.com/products/bolt-action-third-edition-mini-rulebook).
3. **Playing a nation that isn't in the book?** Warlord's [digital resources page](https://warlord-community.warlordgames.com/bolt-action-third-edition-digital-resources/) has free PDF army lists for the other Axis and Allied nations, plus a quick reference sheet and the FAQ and errata. Those lists are being replaced over time by the *Armies of...* books. As of February 2026, all five nations in the rulebook have one, and Italy, the British Commonwealth and France are next.
