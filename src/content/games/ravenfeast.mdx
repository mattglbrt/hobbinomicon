## What is Ravenfeast

Ravenfeast is a free skirmish game for the Viking Age, made by [Little Wars TV](/studios/little-wars-tv/), the YouTube channel about historical wargaming. Each player leads a warband of Viking raiders, and the setting is the raids on Saxon England.

It's built as a way into the hobby. The full book runs 44 pages, but the rules themselves are under six. The rest is a beginner's guide: what historical wargaming is, what you need, how to assemble and paint your Vikings, how to make cheap terrain and a longhouse, and where to go next.

The rules are by [Al Halden](/people/al-halden/), who wrote Ravenfeast in 2010 for his historical wargaming club in Pennsylvania. He based it on a free one-page set called One Page Fantasy Skirmish by John David Slor. After Al died, [Keith Stine](/people/keith-stine/) wrote up the book in his memory, and Little Wars TV published it in 2020.

The PDF is free. Print copies were sold cheap with support from HMGS, the non-profit Historical Miniatures Gaming Society. Little Wars TV's own print run is now sold out.

## How it plays

- **Six phases a turn.** Initiative, Rally, Movement, Missile, Melee, End of Turn. Every player finishes each phase before the next starts.
- **Roll under your stats.** Every Viking has Move, Missile, Melee, Armor and Morale ratings. To hit, roll a D6 equal to or under the attacker's rating. The target then rolls under its Armor to shrug the hit off.
- **Wounds land at the end of the turn.** A wounded Viking gets a Blood Token and keeps fighting until the End of Turn phase. Most figures die from one wound. Jarls and Huskarls are Tough and can take more.
- **A Death Worthy of a Song.** In melee, a to-hit roll of 1 against an Armor roll of 6 earns a free second attack. If that one lands, the wound gets a Raven Token, and every enemy within 6" has to test morale.
- **Shield walls.** Three or more Vikings with shields can lock into a shield wall for +2 Armor and +1 Morale. It moves at half speed, only straight ahead or a quarter move back, and spears in the second rank can fight through the front.
- **Leaders hold the line.** Kill the enemy leader, or wound more than half the warband, and everyone tests morale. Leaders and Heroes lend their Morale to nearby Vikings. Fail a test and the Viking flees and has to rally next turn or be removed.
- **Points, or build your own.** The book gives ready-made Jarls, Huskarls, Berserkers, Hirdmen, Bondi, archers and Thralls, plus an open points system for making your own troop types.
- **Optional extras.** Rune cards add one-off twists. Geld coins buy rerolls and extra Berserkers, and can be looted off a dead leader. There are Saxon lists with cavalry, a big battle option using group bases, and a mythology supplement with trolls, frost giants and draugar.

1. **Download the book.** It's free on the [downloads page](https://www.ravenfeast.com/free-downloads.html), in full colour or a print-friendly black and white version. There's a one-page quick reference sheet too.
2. **Watch the tutorial.** Little Wars TV has a [video walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zIoGbrTVED8) of a full turn.
3. **Try it with paper Vikings.** The downloads page has hand-drawn, double-sided paper figures. About a dozen per player is enough for a first game.
4. **Gather a warband.** The book suggests 12 to 20 figures: a Jarl, two to four Huskarls, six to ten spearmen, two to four archers and a Berserker. Any scale works. The book uses 28mm on 1" square bases. You also need a tape measure, ten D6 and some red, yellow and black markers.
5. **Pick a scenario.** The book has three: Back to the Boats!, Fight for Honor! and Pillage & Burn! More are on the [scenarios page](https://www.ravenfeast.com/scenarios.html), including a big battle scenario for Ashdown.

## Community & reviews

The book ends with a list of Viking miniature makers in 28mm and 15mm, and suggests SAGA, Strandhogg, [Lion Rampant](/games/lion-rampant/) and Dux Britanniarum once you want something deeper. Fan translations are up in French, Polish and Chinese.
