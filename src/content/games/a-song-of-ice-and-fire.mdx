## What is A Song of Ice and Fire: Tabletop Miniatures Game

A Song of Ice and Fire: Tabletop Miniatures Game is a mass battle game from [CMON](/studios/cmon/), based on George R. R. Martin's novels. CMON made it with Dark Sword Miniatures, and it launched on Kickstarter in July 2017 with a Stark vs. Lannister starter set.

The setting is the War of the Five Kings. Each player picks a faction and fields regiments of infantry and cavalry, plus heroes, monsters and war machines. Alongside the fighting there's a political game: your characters sit off the table, pulling strings on a shared Tactics Board.

The game credits Michael Shinall, Fábio Cury and Eric M. Lang with the game design.

There are ten factions, plus the Neutrals:

- **House Stark**
- **House Lannister**
- **The Night's Watch**
- **The Free Folk**
- **House Baratheon**
- **House Targaryen**
- **House Greyjoy**
- **House Martell**
- **House Bolton**
- **The Brotherhood Without Banners**

Neutral units are sellswords and minor houses. You can add them to any army, up to 30% of your points, or field an all-Neutral army.

The game runs in seasons. CMON rebalances the cards every year, and the current printed rules are the Season 6 rulebook. Season 7 card updates went up on the official site in 2026, along with a changelog.

## How it plays

- **Regiments on trays.** An infantry unit is a tray of 12 models in 3 ranks. Cavalry come 4 to a tray in 2 ranks. Heroes, monsters and war machines sit on solo trays.
- **Losing ranks hurts.** Casualties come off the back rank first. Every attack lists three dice values, and the number you roll drops as whole ranks are lost.
- **Alternating activations.** Players take turns activating one unit at a time. Each combat unit gets one action: maneuver, march, retreat, attack or charge. An engaged unit has to attack or retreat.
- **Facing matters.** Each tray has a front, a rear and two flanks. Attacks from the flank give the defender -1 to defence rolls and panic tests, and from the rear it's -2. A charge is a D6 plus the unit's Speed, and a successful charge lets you re-roll attack dice. Roll a 1 and it's a Disorderly Charge.
- **Panic.** After being attacked, a unit takes a panic test: 2D6 against its Morale stat. Fail it and the unit suffers D3 more wounds, with no saves.
- **The Tactics Board.** Your Non-Combat Units, the plotters and politicians, don't go on the table. When they activate they claim one of five zones on a shared board: Crown, Wealth, Tactics, Combat or Maneuver. Each zone triggers its own effect. Some characters also attach to a unit on the table, friend or foe, while they hold their zone.
- **Commanders and the Tactics deck.** Your Commander is free and brings 3 cards of their own. Those join 7 faction cards, two copies of each, to make a 20-card Tactics deck. You refill your hand to 3 each round, play cards off triggers during the game, and the deck isn't replenished when it runs out.
- **Ten game modes.** Each mode sets how you score victory points: holding objectives, a castle siege, secret missions and more. You also score 1 point for every enemy combat unit destroyed. First to 8, 10 or 12 points (for a 30, 40 or 50 point game) wins at the end of a round. If nobody gets there by the end of round 6, the most points wins.

1. **Read the rules for free.** The [Season 6 rulebook](https://asoiaf.cmon.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/S06-Rulebook.pdf) is a free download. It's big, around 76 MB.
2. **Get the current cards.** The [documents page](https://asoiaf.cmon.com/all-documents/) has the Season 7 cards for every faction as print-and-play PDFs, plus the changelog, FAQ, scenarios and tournament guidelines. CMON's War Council app has an army builder and the card library too.
3. **Pick a faction starter set.** Each faction has a starter set with everything to play: the rulebook, cards, Tactics Board, tokens, dice, movement trays, terrain and a starting army. The Stark starter has 56 miniatures, the Free Folk one 44. CMON lists each at $129.99. The models come pre-assembled but unpainted.
4. **Buy through a store.** CMON's US webstore is a trade store for brick-and-mortar shops, so ask your local game store. Some boxes go out of stock, and CMON says reprints happen periodically.
5. **Grow from there.** Unit boxes, hero boxes and attachments add to an army. A 40-point game is the medium size.

## Community & reviews

CMON says the official Facebook page has a community of more than 15,000 people. Competitive play is tracked on ASOIAF Stats, the community site that logs tournaments and faction results.
