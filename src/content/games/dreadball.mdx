## What is DreadBall

DreadBall is a sci-fi sports game from [Mantic Games](/studios/mantic-games/). Mantic calls it "the fast-paced futuristic sports board game." The rulebook calls it "the Galactic Co-Prosperity Sphere's greatest sport": a violent ball game played by humans and aliens from Mantic's Warpath universe, the setting of [Deadzone](/games/deadzone/).

[Jake Thornton](/people/jake-thornton/) designed it. It went to Kickstarter in September 2012 and was funded in two hours.

The current **Second Edition** reached Kickstarter backers at the end of 2017. Mantic rebalanced the teams, added a new fouling system, dropped random events and gave every team a captain. The Second Edition rulebook carries rules and stats for 29 teams, 67 MVPs and Team Captains, plus league play, Giants and multiplayer games.

**Mantic no longer has the miniatures in stock.** The only teams on its DreadBall page are listed as out of stock, and there's no starter set for sale. The rules are still easy to get, though: the core rules and team stats are a free download, and the full rulebook is sold as a digital edition.

## How it plays

- **Six a side.** Each team puts six players on the pitch. Every player is a Guard, a Jack or a Striker. Guards hit hard and usually can't handle the ball, Strikers score, and Jacks do a bit of both.
- **Rushes and actions.** Each turn is a Rush. A team gets five Actions per Rush, spent on things like Run, Sprint, Throw, Catch, Steal and Slam.
- **Scoring Strikes.** Each end of the pitch has three Strike Zones. A player holding the ball in one of them throws it into the Strike Hex. The two nearer zones are worth 1 point and the far one 3, and throwing from the Bonus Hex adds a point.
- **Track the gap, not the total.** The score counter moves towards whoever scored, so it shows the difference. After 14 Rushes the leader wins, and anyone 7 points up wins by a landslide on the spot.
- **Six-sided dice and cards.** Tests use D6s. DreadBall cards and Fan Checks earn Coaching Dice when your players pull off something showy.
- **A robot referee.** The Ref punishes fouls, but a particularly successful one gets the crowd cheering.
- **Leagues.** Teams earn experience and money between games, and you can hire MVPs and Giants.

1. **Read the rules for free.** The [core rules and team stats](https://www.manticgames.com/uncategorised/dreadball-core-rules-and-team-stats/) land in your Mantic downloads once you check out. Mantic also has a free FAQ and errata, captain cards and a team roster sheet on its [DreadBall page](https://www.manticgames.com/dreadball/).
2. **Get the full rulebook.** The [Second Edition rulebook](https://www.manticgames.com/dreadball/books-dreadball/dreadball-2nd-edition-rulebook-digital/) is £20 as a 210-page digital edition.
3. **Find the teams.** Mantic isn't selling the miniatures right now, so you'll need to track down a team elsewhere.
4. **Or play online.** A free fan-made [Vassal module](https://www.manticgames.com/news/how-to-play-dreadball-online-using-vassal/) lets you play DreadBall on a computer.
