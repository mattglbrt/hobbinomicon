## What is Space Weirdos

*Space Weirdos* is a sci-fi skirmish game by [Casey Garske](/people/casey-garske/), published as [Garske Games](/studios/garske-games/). He calls it a "skirmish heartbreaker." His pitch is that a bunch of old-school D&D nerds got into wargaming and found minis games have the same edition creep as RPGs, so he wrote a short set of rules instead.

There's no setting to buy into. Use any minis you have. A warband is five to ten models or so, and there's a points system, but he says you shouldn't worry about it too much.

The whole game fits in one 16-page zine. It was funded on Kickstarter as a Zine Quest project, with 333 backers. Its fantasy sequel is [Sword Weirdos](/games/sword-weirdos/), and by the time that one launched in 2023, Space Weirdos was a Mithral best seller on Wargame Vault.

## How it plays

- **Stats are dice.** A model doesn't have fixed numbers. Each stat is a die type, and effects can push it up or down during a game.
- **Opposed rolls.** The attacker rolls, the defender rolls, and the higher roll wins.
- **Hits go to a table.** A hit sends the target to a table to see what happens, and a big roll makes the result worse. Some results let the target shoot back.
- **Alternating activations.** Players take turns activating one model at a time.
- **Command Points.** A small pool you spend on things like overwatch shots, diving into cover, better aim, or initiative.
- **Sticks, not tape.** All movement and ranges are measured with 5" sticks.
- **All your RPG dice.** Everything except the d20.

## How to start

1. **Get the zine.** $5 or more on [itch.io](https://caseyg.itch.io/space-weirdos), or from [Wargame Vault](https://www.wargamevault.com/en/product/359157/space-weirdos).
2. **Look at what's in the download.** The rules zine, a separate 4-page solo rules PDF, and a PDF with tokens, hobby advice, and a warband sheet.
3. **Pull five to ten minis off the shelf.** Whatever sci-fi models you've got.
4. **Cut some 5" sticks.** That's your measuring kit.
