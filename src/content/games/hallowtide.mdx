## What is Hallowtide

*Hallowtide* is "a black powder eldritch fantasy miniature skirmish game" from [Black Site Studios](/studios/black-site-studios/), the Don't Look Back and Outlaws people. The cover of the quickstart credits [Chris Bunge](/people/chris-bunge/).

The setting is Voyrune. 45 years ago, at the height of an industrial revolution, the dead came back in an event called the Rising and devoured the old world. Most of the wandering dead have gone back to their graves since, but what's left of humanity is still fighting things that crawl out of the edges of nightmare.

You run a crew of hunters sworn to wipe out the forces of darkness, some to make the world safer and some for the silver. Black Site is clear about the tone: "This is not a game of brave heroes and storybook adventure; it is a battle fought by desperate survivors attempting to make the best of an increasingly grim future."

The full game isn't out yet. Black Site says it's "set to launch for preorder in late 2026," and the menu on its store currently reads "Hallowtide (Launching October 30th)."

## How it plays

Only the quickstart is public so far, so this is what Black Site has said about it.

- **Solo and co-op.** The quickstart runs "a solo/cooperative encounter for 1 to 4 players."
- **Generated encounters.** It includes basic rules, encounter generation, pre-generated hunters and monster stats.
- **Pressure on every front.** In Black Site's words, "enemies are deadly, time is limited, and mounting stress can cause even the bravest hunter to lose their reason."

## How to start

1. **Read the free quickstart.** The [Hallowtide Quickstart Preview](https://blacksitestudio.com/products/hallowtide-quickstart-preview) is $0 on the Black Site store. If you'd rather not check out, the PDF is also on their [Discord](https://discord.gg/9VCUAxqCY4).
2. **Watch for the preorder.** Black Site's store lists the launch as October 30th.
