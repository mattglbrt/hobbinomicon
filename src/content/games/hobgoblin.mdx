## What is Hobgoblin

*Hobgoblin* is a rank-and-flank fantasy mass battle game by [Mike Hutchinson](/people/mike-hutchinson/), the designer of *Gaslands*, published by [Electi Studio](/studios/electi-studio/). Big blocks of troops, wheeling into line and crashing together, but finished in under two hours instead of an afternoon.

There are no preset factions. The army list is a toolkit: you give each unit a type, then buy keywords that fit the models you have. Hutchinson says he wanted "a game in which any model I choose to paint is a viable choice on the battlefield."

The setting is the Rotvärlden, a maze of tunnels, caverns and catacombs under a dead, frozen surface. Cursed Artefacts let their bearers, the Haunted, summon whole armies, while the artefact's long-dead maker slowly takes them over. The art is by CROM.

It went through Kickstarter in spring 2023 and a stretch of open beta before the hardcover. In 2024 it won Best Miniatures Rules at the UK Games Expo Judge's Choice Awards.

## How it plays

- **Units are rectangles.** Any tray with an obvious front edge. Footprint sets the size: wide units are 100 to 200mm across, narrow ones 40 to 100mm.
- **Unit type does the heavy lifting.** Light Infantry, Heavy Cavalry, Chariot, War Wagon, Monster, Artillery and the rest each set speed, range and free keywords. Extra keywords cost points.
- **Rock-paper-scissors matchups.** A single chart gives the target number for every attacker against every defender. Heavy Cavalry hits Ranged Infantry on a 2+ and Heavy Infantry on a 4+.
- **Fixed dice pools.** Shooting is 5 dice, combat is 10. Every hit is a point of Doom.
- **Doom breaks units.** At 10 Doom a unit is Broken and removed. Each Broken unit makes friends within 8" panic and take more Doom, so a bad flank can collapse.
- **Movement points.** Each unit gets points equal to its Speed and spends them to advance, shuffle, wheel or reform.
- **Five rounds, six phases.** Initiative, magic, shooting, movement, combat, doom. Most points left on the table wins.
- **Fortune Cards.** You draw one each round, and every unit you lose earns you a Fortune Card or two Mercury Tokens for magic.

The quick start has one rule above the rest, the Rule of Carnage: if the rules are ever unclear, "pick whichever option results in the most carnage for all concerned."

## How to start

1. **Grab the free downloads.** Planet Smasher Games has the [Quick Start rules](https://planetsmashergames.com/hobgoblin/) plus print-and-play Fortune Cards, tokens and paper units, all free.
2. **Build a list.** The [army builder](https://hobgoblin.indietabletop.club/) is free. A small army is 2,000 points.
3. **Use what's on your shelf.** Any fantasy army works. Paper units or cut card will do while you paint.
4. **Buy the book when you're hooked.** Electi sells the [PDF](https://www.electi-studio.com/store/p/hobgoblin-pdf-only) for $19.99 and the hardcover from $45. The printed Fortune Deck is $15.49: 66 cards covering the Fortune, Abyssal Sorcery and Scenario decks plus quick reference cards.
5. **Clear a 4' × 3' table.** Set some fantasy terrain on it and deploy at least 16" apart.
