Nic Wright's rules are published through his own [Irregular Wars Wargaming](/studios/irregular-wars/) and through Ganesha Games. He wrote [Devilry Afoot](/games/devilry-afoot/), a folk horror skirmish game for solo and co-op play, and [Fantastic Battles](/games/fantastic-battles/), a fast-play fantasy mass-battle game.

Irregular Wars describes his rules as using randomised initiative and "a degree of command friction," written to fit whatever period or setting the player wants.
