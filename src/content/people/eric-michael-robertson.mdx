Eric Michael Robertson is one of the creators of [1490 DOOM](/games/1490-doom/), the skirmish game published by [Buer Games](/studios/buer-games/).

He and Buer Games co-founder Cody Taylor produced *The Grim & The Dark*, a documentary about grimdark in miniatures.
