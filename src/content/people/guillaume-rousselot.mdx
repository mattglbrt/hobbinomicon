Guillaume Rousselot writes games as Noodle Wargames. His game [Pillage](/games/pillage/) started as a French game, and Victrix publishes the English edition, translated by Matt Moran. He also runs the French edition and a work-in-progress army builder at [pillagewargame.com](https://www.pillagewargame.com/), and the game's Discord.
