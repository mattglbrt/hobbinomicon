Matt Burns writes games for [Black Site Studios](/studios/black-site-studios/). His [Don't Look Back](/games/dont-look-back/) was the studio's first game in 2020, and his other books for Black Site include *Breacher* and *Violent Dark*. He wrote [Outlaws: The Curse of Sherwood Forest](/games/outlaws-the-curse-of-sherwood-forest/), a solo and co-op Robin Hood game with a dark fantasy twist.
