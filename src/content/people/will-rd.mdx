Will RD is a writer and designer from Wales, and the maker of [Forbidden Psalm](/games/forbidden-psalm/), the skirmish game set in the world of MÖRK BORG. The expansions are Will's too, including *KillSampleProcess*, which takes the game into CY_BORG.

Other games on Will's [itch.io page](https://impioussaint.itch.io/) include the folk horror RPG *Run From the Dark*, *Psalms of Ragnarök*, *Forbidden Tales*, *MORK BOLL*, *HORSE WAR*, and *Childhood*.
