Andrea Sfiligoi wrote and illustrated [Song of Blades and Heroes](/games/song-of-blades-and-heroes/), the fast fantasy skirmish game [Ganesha Games](/studios/ganesha-games/) first published in 2007, and its updated version, Advanced Song of Blades and Heroes. He also designed [Sellswords & Spellslingers](/games/sellswords-and-spellslingers/), a solo and co-op fantasy miniatures game released in 2018.
