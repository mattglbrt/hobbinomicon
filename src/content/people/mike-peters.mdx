Mike Peters designed [Here's the Ruckus](/games/heres-the-ruckus/), published free with *Wargames Illustrated* issue 437 by [Wargames Illustrated](/studios/wargames-illustrated/). It started as an idea for downsizing *Never Mind the Billhooks*, and he playtested it at Bristol Independent Gaming and Bristol Wargaming Society before release.

His follow-up, *Assassins and Templars: A Ruckus Game*, uses the same rules engine for skirmishes set during the Crusades.
