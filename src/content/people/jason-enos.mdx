Jason Enos is the lead developer and designer of [Bushido](/games/bushido/) at [GCT Studios](/studios/gct-studios/). He started as a GCT playtester, won the first Bushido Grand Masters, and took over development when the previous developer stepped down. Before that he ran organised play in Europe for Privateer Press and worked as a developer on Fallout and Elder Scrolls for [Modiphius](/studios/modiphius/).

One of his changes for the current edition was doubling the points scale, so a standard game went from 50 Rice to 100.
