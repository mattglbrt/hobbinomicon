Carlos Torres Rodríguez co-founded Corvus Belli in 2001 in Galicia, with Fernando Liste and Pilar Rivero. He trained in sculpture restoration and led the visual design and miniatures side of Infinity, which he's credited with creating alongside Gutier Lusquiños, Alberto Abal, and Fernando Liste. More recently he's a design lead on Warcrow, Corvus Belli's fantasy wargame.
