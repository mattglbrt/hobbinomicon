Mike Hutchinson designs tabletop wargames as Planet Smasher Games. His games include *Gaslands*, *A Billion Suns*, *Mystic Skies*, *Perilous Tales* and the fantasy mass battle game [Hobgoblin](/games/hobgoblin/), which [Electi Studio](/studios/electi-studio/) publishes under license.

He co-hosts the Rule of Carnage podcast and lives in the UK.
