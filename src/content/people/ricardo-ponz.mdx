Ricardo Ponz is credited with the original idea and creation of [FIRE! Modern Combat](/games/fire-modern-combat/), published by [SEN Games](/studios/sen-games/). He designed and wrote the game with [Aleix Gordo Hostau](/people/aleix-gordo-hostau/), and the miniatures in the game's photos come from their two collections.
