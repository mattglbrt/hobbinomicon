Charles Murton spent his whole career in the Civil Service, working for the Ministry of Defence, including professional wargaming for the military. He took early retirement in 2016. In Her Majesty's Name was his first professional writing job, and role-playing games are his first love.

He co-owns [The Ministry of Gentlemanly Warfare](/studios/ministry-of-gentlemanly-warfare/) with [Craig Cartmell](/people/craig-cartmell/), and the two wrote [Blood Eagle](/games/blood-eagle/), its Dark Ages skirmish game. He's also a competitive target shooter, which is why the firearms in their rules are his department.
