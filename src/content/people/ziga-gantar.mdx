Žiga Gantar founded [Freecompany](/studios/freecompany/) and is its CEO and creative director. He's credited as a writer of the [Eldfall Chronicles](/games/eldfall-chronicles/) core rules with [Tjaša Gaber](/people/tjasa-gaber/), and as one of the game's developers. He also did character and landscape art for the rulebook.
