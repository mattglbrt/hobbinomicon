Gav Thorpe is the narrative lead on [Zeo Genesis](/games/zeo-genesis/), published by [Best Hobby](/studios/best-hobby/). He's also credited with game development and with writing for the game's World Anvil wiki. He was a Games Workshop writer before this.

He also co-wrote the [Carnevale](/games/carnevale/) rulebook for [TTCombat](/studios/ttcombat/).
