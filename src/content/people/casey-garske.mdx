Casey Garske designs indie games as Garske Games. He made the skirmish wargame [Space Weirdos](/games/space-weirdos/) and its fantasy sequel, [Sword Weirdos](/games/sword-weirdos/), and he wrote the roleplaying game *Stay Frosty*.

His games are on his [itch.io page](https://caseyg.itch.io/) as Casey G.
