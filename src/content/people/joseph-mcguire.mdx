Joey McGuire runs World's End Publishing and wrote [This Is Not a Test](/games/this-is-not-a-test/), a post-apocalyptic skirmish game that debuted in 2015. It was his first solo publication. The second edition is published by [Black Site Studios](/studios/black-site-studios/) under licence, and McGuire still owns the game.

He got into miniatures in 1997, after walking into a game store and finding the Games Workshop catalogue, and has been painting, building terrain and writing his own rules since. He has also worked on projects for Rattrap Productions.
