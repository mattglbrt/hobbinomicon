Giacomo "Jack" Pantalone has gone by The Lazy Forger online since 2009. He co-created [Full Spectrum Dominance](/games/full-spectrum-dominance/) with [Federico Valsecchi](/people/federico-valsecchi/), and the two also wrote *Round of Fire*.

The game's 3D-printable miniatures and terrain are sold through [The Lazy Forger store on MyMiniFactory](https://www.myminifactory.com/users/TheLazyForger), and he posts each rulebook update on [the FSD site](https://fsd-wargame.com/blog/).
