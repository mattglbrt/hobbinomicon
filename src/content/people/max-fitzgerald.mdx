Max FitzGerald is a British artist, and the designer and illustrator of [Turnip28](/games/turnip28/). The rules have been free on Patreon since 2020, and the current version, v19, credits Gaetano Ferrara as game designer alongside Max.

Max is also working on *Swill*, an official Turnip28 skirmish game still in playtest.
