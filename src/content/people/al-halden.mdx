Al Halden wrote the rules for [Ravenfeast](/games/ravenfeast/) in 2010, for his historical miniature wargaming club in Pennsylvania. He believed the best wargaming rules were short, and based the game on a free one-page set called One Page Fantasy Skirmish by John David Slor.

[Little Wars TV](/studios/little-wars-tv/) published Ravenfeast as a free book in 2020, dedicated to his memory.
