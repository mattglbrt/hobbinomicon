Joseph A. McCullough designed [Frostgrave](/games/frostgrave/), the wizard warband game published by Osprey Games, and built a family of games on the same d20 engine. That family includes Frostgrave: Ghost Archipelago, Stargrave, Oathmark, and The Silver Bayonet.

He also designed [Rangers of Shadow Deep](/games/rangers-of-shadow-deep/), a solo and co-op adventure game he self-published in 2018. Modiphius now publishes the core book, and he still self-publishes most of its missions. His other games include Deathship One, Operation: Last Train, and Hairfoot Jousting.
