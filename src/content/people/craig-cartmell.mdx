Craig Cartmell has been gaming since the mid-1970s and writing his own rules for about thirty years. His first successes were FUBAR, one-page rules for small unit actions, and In the Emperor's Name, a skirmish game for Warhammer 40K. That second one brought Osprey to him with the offer to write steampunk rules, which became In Her Majesty's Name.

He now co-owns [The Ministry of Gentlemanly Warfare](/studios/ministry-of-gentlemanly-warfare/) with [Charles Murton](/people/charles-murton/), and the two wrote [Blood Eagle](/games/blood-eagle/), its Dark Ages skirmish game. He lives in rural Wales.
