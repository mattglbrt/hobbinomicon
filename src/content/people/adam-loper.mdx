Adam Loper, "Uncle Atom" of Tabletop Minions, co-founded [Snarling Badger Studios](/studios/snarling-badger-studios/) with [Vince Venturella](/people/vince-venturella/). The two made [Repent! Ye Foolish Gods](/games/repent-ye-foolish-gods/), a mini-agnostic solo and co-op skirmish game about a warband out for revenge on the dark gods.
