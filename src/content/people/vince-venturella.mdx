Vince Venturella co-founded [Snarling Badger Studios](/studios/snarling-badger-studios/) with [Adam Loper](/people/adam-loper/) of Tabletop Minions. The two made [Repent! Ye Foolish Gods](/games/repent-ye-foolish-gods/), a mini-agnostic solo and co-op skirmish game about a warband out for revenge on the dark gods.
