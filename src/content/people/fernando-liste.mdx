Fernando Liste co-founded Corvus Belli in 2001 in Galicia, with Carlos Torres and Pilar Rivero. He's a sculptor, and one of the four people credited with creating Infinity, alongside Gutier Lusquiños, Alberto Abal, and Carlos Torres.
