Lewis Clarke co-wrote the [Carnevale](/games/carnevale/) rulebook with [Gav Thorpe](/people/gav-thorpe/) for [TTCombat](/studios/ttcombat/), and the two returned to write the campaign expansion *Blood on the Water*. Goonhammer named him as Carnevale's lead developer in 2020.
