Chris McDowall designed [The Doomed](/games/the-doomed/), a horror-hunting skirmish game published by [Osprey Games](/studios/osprey-games/) in 2023.

Most of his work is roleplaying games, which he calls his career. His site, [Bastionland](https://www.bastionland.com/), lists *Into the Odd*, *Electric Bastionland*, and *Mythic Bastionland* among his games.
