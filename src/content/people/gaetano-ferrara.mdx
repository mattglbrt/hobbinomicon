Gaetano Ferrara is credited as the creator of the [OnePageRules](/studios/one-page-rules/) games. The Age of Fantasy: Skirmish rulebook lists him for both "Created By" and "Game Design," and the core rules for [Age of Fantasy: Quest](/games/age-of-fantasy-quest/) and Grimdark Future carry the same credit.

His games on this site: [Age of Fantasy: Skirmish](/games/age-of-fantasy-skirmish/) and [Age of Fantasy: Quest](/games/age-of-fantasy-quest/).
