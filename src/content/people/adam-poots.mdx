Adam Poots created the co-op campaign board game [Kingdom Death: Monster](/games/kingdom-death-monster/) and publishes it through his company, [Kingdom Death](/studios/kingdom-death/) (Adam Poots Games, LLC). The game first came out in 2015 after a 2012 Kickstarter.
