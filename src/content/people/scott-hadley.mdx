Scott Hadley designed [Of Oil and Iron](/games/of-oil-and-iron/) and sculpts its Rigs, which he sells as 3D-printable files through [Scoutsifer Studio](/studios/scoutsifer-studio/). The game went through an alpha, several betas, and a Kickstarter before Version 1 came out in July 2026.
