Josh Qualtieri is the creator of the Quar. He is credited with rules design, digital art and setting on the 2009 first edition of [This Quar's War](/games/this-quars-war/), and with art, layout and world design on the second edition, The Long War, which he ran on Kickstarter. In the first edition's design notes he writes that the Quar "have been a part of my life for at least twenty-five years."
