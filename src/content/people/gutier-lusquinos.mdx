Gutier Lusquiños Rodríguez is one of the four people credited with creating Infinity, alongside Alberto Abal, Fernando Liste, and Carlos Torres. He's a partner at Corvus Belli and looks after the worldbuilding and background of the Human Sphere, the unit profiles and army lists, and the rules themselves.
