Peter Vigors, who goes by OwlShield, writes [Necropolis28](/games/necropolis-28/) on his own. He's been developing it in the open since early 2022, with free rules, a Patreon for the official STLs, and a very active Discord. The rules have always been free and he's said they always will be.
