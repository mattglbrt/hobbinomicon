Andy Hobday wrote [The Barons' War](/games/the-barons-war/), the medieval skirmish game now published in its Second Edition by [Wargames Atlantic](/studios/wargames-atlantic/). His other games include *Mortal Gods* and *Gangs of Rome*.

He started out in Games Workshop's retail stores, moved to Warlord Games in 2011, and in 2017 bought the UK division of Footsore Miniatures. In 2025 he became Wargames Atlantic's first Creative Director, running development of its own games and its partner games.
