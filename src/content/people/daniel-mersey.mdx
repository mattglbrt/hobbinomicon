Daniel Mersey wrote [Lion Rampant](/games/lion-rampant/), the medieval wargame from [Osprey Games](/studios/osprey-games/), and the games that grew out of it: [Dragon Rampant](/games/dragon-rampant/) for fantasy and *Xenos Rampant* for sci-fi. His other Osprey rulebooks include *Dux Bellorum*, *The Men Who Would Be Kings*, *The Pikeman's Lament*, *Bugles to the West*, and *Rebels and Patriots* (with Michael Leck).

He's been writing books, gaming articles and rulesets since the 1990s. Lion Rampant and Dragon Rampant were both Origins Award nominees, *Lion Rampant: Second Edition* won the 2023 Origins Award for Best Miniatures Game, and *Rebels and Patriots* won a UK Games Expo Judges' Award. In 2021 he was appointed the University of Edinburgh's first Games Designer in Residence.
