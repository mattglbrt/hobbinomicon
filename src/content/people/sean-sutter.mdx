Sean Sutter runs [Metal King Studio](/studios/metal-king-studio/) on his own. He designed [Relicblade](/games/relicblade/), its fantasy skirmish game, and [Sludge](/games/sludge/), a fantasy wargame of line infantry, knights and dark magic in a black powder age. He sculpts the official Sludge miniatures and conversion heads himself.
