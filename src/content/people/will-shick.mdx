Will Shick is credited as lead game designer on [Star Wars: Shatterpoint](/games/star-wars-shatterpoint/) from [Atomic Mass Games](/studios/atomic-mass-games/). The core rulebook also lists him as the studio's Director of Product Development.

He is also credited as lead game designer on the Earth's Mightiest Core Set rulebook for [Marvel: Crisis Protocol](/games/marvel-crisis-protocol/).
