Brett Evans is the designer of [Brawl Arcane 28](/games/brawl-arcane-28/), a wizard-duel miniatures game he developed in public on itch.io through 2022, reaching v1.0 that October. He goes by brettfp on itch and @quarantine_miniatures on Instagram. He's named Turnip28, The Weald, and Blood Bowl as the games that inspired it.
