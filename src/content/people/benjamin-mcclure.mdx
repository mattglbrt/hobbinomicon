Benjamin McClure, from Portland, Oregon, made [Cyber Savage](/games/cyber-savage/) with his brother [Jared McClure](/people/jared-mcclure/). The two wrote the rules together, and Benjamin ran the game's 2025 Kickstarter and publishes the rulebook PDF.
