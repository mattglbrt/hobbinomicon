Stephen Hupfer wrote the words and rules for [Flames of Orion](/games/flames-of-orion/), a miniature-agnostic mech skirmish game published by [Under the Dice](/studios/under-the-dice/). Per the book's thanks, the game started out as "just a sheet of notebook paper."
