Leandros Mavrokefalos is credited as game designer on the core rules of [Conquest: The Last Argument of Kings](/games/conquest-last-argument-of-kings/), [Para Bellum](/studios/para-bellum/)'s mass battle game. The June 2026 [Conquest: First Blood](/games/conquest-first-blood/) rulebook credits him with graphic design and as project manager.
