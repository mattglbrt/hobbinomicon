Graham Davey runs [Grey For Now Games](/studios/grey-for-now-games/). He is the lead writer of the samurai skirmish game *Test of Honour*, and his games also include the WWII commando game *02 Hundred Hours* and [Guards of Traitor's Toll](/games/guards-of-traitors-toll/), a skirmish adventure game about the city guard.

Before going out on his own he worked on Room 17 Games, Warlord Games, Battle Games in Middle-Earth and White Dwarf.
