Doug Cundall created [Hametsu](/games/hametsu/), a solo and co-op monster hunting game set in a post-apocalyptic feudal Japan, published by [Black Site Studios](/studios/black-site-studios/). He also wrote its expansion *The Ryu & The Rain*.
