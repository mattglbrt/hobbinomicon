Ivan Sorensen designed [Five Leagues from the Borderlands](/games/five-leagues-from-the-borderlands/), the solo and co-op fantasy adventure wargame. It first came out in 2018 from his own label, Nordic Weasel Games, and Modiphius publishes the Third Edition, its first time in print.

Its sister game is *Five Parsecs From Home*, his solo sci-fi wargame, which Modiphius also publishes.
