James Morris wrote [Midgard Heroic Battles](/games/midgard/), published by [Reisswitz Press](/studios/reisswitz-press/), the TooFatLardies imprint. The publisher calls him a "veteran gamer." His site, Mogsymakes, hosts free [Midgard resources](https://mogsymakes.net/resources-for-midgard-heroic-battles/): a play sheet, errata, force rosters and extra force lists.
