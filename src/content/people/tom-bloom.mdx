Tom Bloom is a comic author and game designer based in Atlanta, best known for the webcomic *Kill Six Billion Demons* and for co-creating the Lancer RPG with Miguel Lopez-Hall. Under his own label, [CHASM](/studios/chasm/), he designed [Magnagothica: Maleghast](/games/maleghast/), a necromancer skirmish game that came out on itch.io in October 2023, and the horror TTRPG CAIN.
