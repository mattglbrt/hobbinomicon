Mark Latham designed [The Elder Scrolls: Call to Arms](/games/elder-scrolls-call-to-arms/), published by [Modiphius](/studios/modiphius/) in 2020. Modiphius's announcement says he reworked the system behind [Fallout: Wasteland Warfare](/games/fallout-wasteland-warfare/) for the Elder Scrolls setting, and names his earlier work on [The Walking Dead: All Out War](/games/walking-dead-all-out-war/) and as an editor of White Dwarf. He also wrote the game's design blogs, including the one on Delve Mode.
