James Sheahan is credited as the designer of [Fallout: Wasteland Warfare](/games/fallout-wasteland-warfare/), the Fallout miniatures wargame published by [Modiphius](/studios/modiphius/) in 2018. His name heads the credits of the free Rules of Play, the Getting Acclimated tutorial booklet, and the AI and Co-op handbooks.
