Malev writes indie wargames as Malev Da Shinobi, and made [Greathelm](/games/greathelm/), the micro-skirmish knight game illustrated by Sean Sutter. Malev also started the One Box Wargame challenge.

Other games on Malev's [itch.io page](https://malev-da-shinobi.itch.io/) include *DEMON BLITZ*, *DOOM SPRUES*, *MJO*, and *Convict Combat*.
