Alessio Cavatore was born in Turin and moved to Nottingham in 1995 to work for Games Workshop. There he wrote supplements for Warhammer Fantasy Battle, headed up The Lord of the Rings Strategy Battle Game, and co-designed [Mordheim](/games/mordheim/) with Tuomas Pirinen and Rick Priestley. In 2004 he took charge of the rules for Warhammer, Warhammer 40,000 and The Lord of the Rings, and in 2006 he wrote the rules for a new edition of Warhammer.

He developed [Bolt Action](/games/bolt-action/) with Rick Priestley for [Warlord Games](/studios/warlord-games/), and is the credited author of its Third Edition rulebook (2024). He also designed Kings of War for Mantic Games and is the lead game designer on Para Bellum's *Conquest*.
