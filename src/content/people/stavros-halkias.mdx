Stavros Halkias founded [Para Bellum](/studios/para-bellum/). The rules of [Conquest: The Last Argument of Kings](/games/conquest-last-argument-of-kings/) say the game was "conceived and created" by him, and credit him with additional system development. The [Conquest: First Blood](/games/conquest-first-blood/) rulebook names him as "Founder, Creative Director and Lorekeeper of Eä."
