Keith Stine wrote [Ravenfeast](/games/ravenfeast/), the free Viking Age skirmish game from [Little Wars TV](/studios/little-wars-tv/). The rules are Al Halden's, from his and Keith's wargaming club in Pennsylvania, and Keith wrote them up into the 2020 book in Al's memory.
