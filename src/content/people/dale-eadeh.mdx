Dale Eadeh designed [Oasis: Tactical Salvage Reclamation](/games/oasis/), a 6mm sci-fi wargame published by [Collins Epic Wargames](/studios/collins-epic-wargames/). He designed its miniatures as well as the rules. Oasis is his third game design and his first published one.

He owns and designs for Hardware Studios, a shop focused on 6mm. He's also a painter and designer/illustrator for BattleTech. By day he's a creative director in experiential marketing.

The Oasis setting took shape over about a decade of conversations and kitbashing sessions with his friend Jason Kurzweil, an architect. The official site says it draws on 80s sci-fi and on Eadeh's time as a classics student on archaeological digs in Cyprus.
