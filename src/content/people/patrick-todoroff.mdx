Patrick Todoroff writes fiction and designs tabletop miniatures games, and self-publishes them on Wargame Vault and Amazon as P. Todoroff CCWC. His games include [Scrapjacks](/games/scrapjacks/), *Exploit Zero*, *Nightwatch*, *Insurgent Earth*, and two Osprey books, [Zona Alfa](/games/zona-alfa/) and [When Nightmares Come](/games/when-nightmares-come/).

Most of them are cooperative, miniature-agnostic and low model count. He posts design notes and battle reports on his blog, [Stalker7](https://stalker7.com/).
